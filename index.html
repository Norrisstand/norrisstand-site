Norris Stand Drive Folder Sync

Windows desktop utility

Your folders, safely mirrored to Google Drive.

Drive Folder Sync watches the local folders you choose and uploads new or updated files to the Google Drive destination you select.

01

Choose folders

Select one or more Windows folders. Each source is kept in its own matching subfolder under your chosen Drive destination.

02

Connect Google Drive

Sign in directly with Google and select the destination folder. The app requests access only to files and folders used with this app.

03

Keep working

The app runs in the Windows notification area and retries files that are temporarily unavailable, such as a document open in another program.

Privacy by design

Drive Folder Sync runs on your computer. It does not operate a hosted file-storage service or send your file contents to Norris Stand. Google Drive transfers occur between the app, your computer, and your Google account after you authorize them.
