Drive Folder Sync Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Drive Folder Sync handles information when you use the Norris Stand Windows desktop application.

Information the app stores locally

Drive Folder Sync stores the folder mappings, Google Drive destination selections, application preferences, local activity logs, and Google authorization tokens that you choose to save on your Windows computer so the app can continue syncing.

Google Drive access

After you authorize the application with Google, it uses the Google Drive permission needed to create, update, and organize the files and folders you selected for syncing. The app accesses Google Drive only through Google's APIs and only after your authorization.

Multiple saved accounts

If you add more than one Google account inside the app, each account's saved sign-in, destination folder, and source-folder mappings are stored locally on that PC. Norris Stand does not receive a hosted copy of those settings.

File contents

The app reads local files solely to upload or update them in the Google Drive destination you selected. Norris Stand does not receive, store, sell, share, or use the contents of your synced files. Transfers occur directly between the application, your computer, and your Google Drive account.

Security

Keep your Windows account and Google account secure. You can revoke the app's Google access at any time from your Google Account security settings. Removing the app's local settings or revoking its access may require you to authorize it again.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the application changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@norrisstand.com.
