Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Drive Folder Sync handles information when you use its Windows desktop application.

Information the app uses

Drive Folder Sync uses the local folder paths, Google Drive folder selection, and application settings you choose. The app stores its settings and Google authorization token locally on your Windows computer so it can continue syncing.

Google Drive access

After you authorize the application with Google, it uses the Google Drive permission needed to create, update, and organize the files and folders you select for syncing. The application accesses Google Drive only through Google's APIs and only after your authorization.

File contents

The app reads local files solely to upload or update them in the Google Drive destination you selected. Norris Stand does not receive, store, sell, share, or use the contents of your synced files. Transfers occur directly between the application and your Google Drive account.

Security

Keep your Windows account and Google account secure. You can revoke the app's Google access at any time from your Google Account security settings. Removing the app's local settings or revoking its access may require you to authorize it again.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the application changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to norrisstand@gmail.com.
