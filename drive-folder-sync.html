Norris Stand Drive Folder Sync

Windows desktop application

Keep selected Windows folders copied and up to date in Google Drive.

Drive Folder Sync watches the local folders you choose and uploads new or updated files to the Google Drive destinations you set. Each saved Google account can keep its own destination and folder mappings—without installing Google Drive for desktop.

Use it as one layer of protection

  • Drive Folder Sync is a sync and upload tool, not a replacement for a complete backup plan.
  • Syncs new and updated files without requiring Google Drive for desktop.
  • Keeps the upload queue on the user's PC and retries temporary failures automatically.
  • Lets each Google account keep its own destination and local folder assignments.
  • Leaves delete actions alone so local file removal does not erase the Drive copy.
  • Provides local logs, a quick start guide, a clean way to fully exit the app, and official update checks from Norris Stand.
