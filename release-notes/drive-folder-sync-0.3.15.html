Drive Folder Sync 0.3.15

A verified Google Drive connection hotfix.

Published on August 25, 2026.

Download 0.3.15 Checksum

What changed

  • Includes the Google client modules and the official Drive v3 discovery document needed when a customer adds or reconnects a Google account.
  • Fixes the packaged-app Google connection errors that could appear in earlier installer candidates.
  • Adds a package-integrity build gate that verifies those Google components and the bundled OAuth asset before an installer can be published.

For current customers

Install the current release from Norris Stand before adding or reconnecting a Google account. Existing saved Google-account connections and folder mappings are preserved.
