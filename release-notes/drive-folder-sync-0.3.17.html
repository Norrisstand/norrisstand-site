Drive Folder Sync 0.3.17

Better support evidence when something goes wrong.

Published on August 25, 2026.

Download 0.3.17 Checksum

What changed

  • Replaces the plain activity-log export with a customer-chosen redacted support-bundle ZIP.
  • The bundle includes redacted activity logs and a diagnostic summary, but excludes Google OAuth tokens, activation tokens, account emails, full local paths, and file contents.
  • Google connection, activation, folder-picker, destination, monitoring, update, and sync failures are now recorded in the local activity log for support review.

For customers

If Norris Stand support asks for diagnostics, use Export support bundle in the app or tray menu and attach the ZIP file to your support email.
