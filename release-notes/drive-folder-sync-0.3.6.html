Drive Folder Sync 0.3.6

Separate Google-account slots from device seats.

Published on August 24, 2026.

Download 0.3.6 Checksum

Highlights

  • Licenses now track Google-account slots separately from device seats.
  • Standard licensing now includes 2 devices and 2 Google accounts by default.
  • Each license still enforces the device-seat limit independently from the Google-account limit.

Included improvements

  • The portal now shows how many Google-account slots are in use for each customer.
  • Admins can review registered Google accounts and release an occupied slot when needed.
  • The desktop app now registers connected Google accounts against the portal and blocks extra unique accounts once the license limit is reached.
