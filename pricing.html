Onboarding

A guided first setup

Norris Stand helps new customers start with the right license, limits, and support path from day one.

Start securely online and Norris Stand will send the setup link so you can activate your Windows PC, connect Google, and begin monitoring with confidence.
  • Use the customer portal from the welcome email sent after checkout
  • Each Windows PC still needs approval before monitoring begins
  • Questions about pricing or account limits go through hello@norrisstand.com

How to think about the limits

  • A device seat is one approved Windows PC.
  • A Google account slot is one approved Google account connected under the customer license.
  • Source-folder mappings live underneath those approved accounts and do not have their own separate per-folder fee.
  • Deleting a local file still does not delete the matching Google Drive file.
FAQ

Do I need Google Drive for desktop?

No. Drive Folder Sync connects through Google's APIs and can upload directly to Google Drive without installing Google Drive for desktop.

FAQ

What if I need more than 5 Google accounts?

Contact Norris Stand before rollout. The current launch model hard-stops at 5 Google accounts per license so the product is not abused or overshared.

FAQ

Can I start with a conversation instead of guessing?

Yes. If you want help deciding whether the standard plan is enough, email hello@norrisstand.com and Norris Stand can guide the onboarding setup.
